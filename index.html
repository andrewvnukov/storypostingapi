StoryPostingApi

A publishing tool for creators: upload, schedule and publish your own videos and photo posts to your own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. You connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and approve the requested permissions.
  2. You upload a video or images and write the caption in the editor.
  3. Before publishing, the app loads your account settings from TikTok and shows the privacy level options, comment / duet / stitch settings, and the commercial-content disclosure switches that your account allows.
  4. You press Post. The app sends the content to TikTok and shows the publish status; nothing is ever posted without that action.

What it does not do

  • It does not access accounts other than the one you connect.
  • It does not read or collect other users' data.
  • It does not generate engagement, followers, views or comments.
