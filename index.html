StoryPostingApi

A story-video studio for creators: turn a Reddit story into a narrated vertical video, preview it, and publish it to your own TikTok account — right away or on a schedule — through the official TikTok Content Posting API.

How it works

  1. You connect your TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit and approve the requested permissions.
  2. You paste a Reddit post link or your own text, choose a background clip, a narration voice, a speaking speed and a caption style.
  3. The app renders a 1080×1920 video with burned-in captions and an opening headline card, and plays it back so you can check it before anything leaves your computer.
  4. Before publishing, the app loads your account settings from TikTok and shows the privacy level options, comment / duet / stitch settings, and the commercial-content disclosure switches that your account allows. Videos with a synthetic voice are marked as AI-generated content.
  5. You press Publish, now or for a chosen time. Nothing is ever posted without that action.

Monitoring

For each account you connect, the app shows followers, likes and the view, like, comment and share counts of its recent videos, so you can see how the published stories performed. Only accounts that authorized the app are read — there is no tracking of anyone else's channel.

What it does not do

  • It does not access accounts other than the one you connect.
  • It does not read or collect other users' data.
  • It does not generate engagement, followers, views or comments.
